Why measurement error matters
  • Every measurement has an uncertainty
  • Random error: scatters readings around the true value
    • Reduced by repeating the measurement
  • Systematic error shifts all readings the same way
  • Example: a thermometer that reads 1 degree high
    • Fixed by calibration
  • Report results as value ± uncertainty

Field kit versus lab instrument
Field kit
  • Easy to repeat
  • Cheap equipment
  • Results in minutes
  • Good for teaching
Lab instrument
  • Low precision
  • Affected by wind
  • Needs trained observer
  • Hard to automate

Summary
  1. Every measurement has an uncertainty.
  2. Repeat measurements to reduce random error.
  3. Calibrate instruments to catch systematic error.
  4. Always report units and uncertainty.

Problem set 3 solutions

Problem 1. A cart of mass 2 kg accelerates at 3 m/s². What is the net force?

Solution: F = ma = 2 × 3 = 6 N.

Problem 2. A ball is dropped from 20 m. How long does it fall?

Solution: t = √(2h/g) = √(40/9.8) ≈ 2.0 s.

\section*{Matrix forms}
A single matrix in parentheses:
\[ A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}. \]
A single matrix in brackets, three by three:
\[ B = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & -1 & 0 \\ -1 & 2 & -1 \\ 0 & -1 & 2 \end{bmatrix}. \]
A determinant:
\[ \det C = \begin{vmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{vmatrix} = ad - bc. \]
A matrix times a vector:
\[ \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 1 & 3 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \\ z \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 4 \\ 5 \end{pmatrix}. \]
An augmented matrix:
\[ \left[\begin{array}{ccc|c} 1 & 2 & 1 & 4 \\ 0 & 1 & -1 & 1 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & 2 \end{array}\right] \]

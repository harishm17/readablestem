How to Submit Assignments

This handout explains how to turn in work for BIO 150. Late work policies are in the syllabus.

Steps

  1. Open the course site and sign in with your campus account.
  2. Choose Assignments from the left menu.
  3. Select the assignment named in the table below.
  4. Upload a single PDF and press Submit.
  5. Check that the confirmation page shows a time stamp.

If Something Goes Wrong

  • If the upload fails, try a different browser before emailing the instructor.
  • Keep a copy of your file and a screenshot of any error message.
  • Email the teaching assistant before the deadline with the file attached.

Questions about this process can go to the teaching assistants during office hours.

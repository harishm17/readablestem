ENVS 101: Introduction to Environmental Systems

Fall 2026 · Mondays 10:00 to 11:50 a.m. · Hartwell Hall 210 · 4 credits

Course Description

This course introduces the physical, chemical and biological processes that shape the natural environment. Students learn to measure environmental quantities, to read and draw graphs of field data, and to explain how energy and matter move through ecosystems.

Learning Outcomes

By the end of the course you should be able to:

  1. Describe the major nutrient cycles and the human activities that change them.
  2. Collect field measurements with appropriate units and an estimate of error.
  3. Build and interpret simple population growth models.
  4. Communicate findings in a short written report and an oral presentation.

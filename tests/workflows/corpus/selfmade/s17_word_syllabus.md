# Course Information

This course introduces statistical thinking for students in biology, health and environmental science. Lectures meet Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 9:00 a.m. in Science Hall 104.[^1]

[^1]: Lecture recordings are posted within 24 hours of each class.

## Prerequisites

- Precalculus or equivalent placement score
- Basic familiarity with spreadsheets

## What You Will Learn

1. Summarize data with tables, graphs and numerical measures.
2. Use probability to describe chance variation.
3. Estimate population quantities with confidence intervals.
4. Test claims with hypothesis tests and interpret p-values carefully.

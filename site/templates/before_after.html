Before and after

The same equations, as a screen reader user would meet them in the PDF and in the HTML.

How to read this page

  • The readings are made by the tool's own speech rules. They are not recordings of NVDA, JAWS or VoiceOver, and real screen readers differ from one another.
  • “Before” is the PDF's own text layer, read one character at a time. These PDFs are untagged print-outs of OpenStax web pages. A PDF with tags, or one with alt text on its equations, would give a different “before”.
  • The equations were chosen to show different kinds of maths. They were not picked at random. They show what the HTML can do, not how often it gets an equation right. For that, see the numbers on the home page.
  • The “after” is the tool's proposal. Where a proposal is wrong, a person is meant to catch it on the review screen. Each equation links to its example.

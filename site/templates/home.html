a11y-stem

a11y-stem helps instructors turn a STEM course PDF into an accessible web page. It also shows a person which parts of the result to check.

This site is a read-only demo. It needs no sign-in. Every result on it was made earlier and is stored with the site. Nothing here sends a document to a model.

What it is

An instructor uploads a PDF. a11y-stem reads it and returns one self-contained HTML file.

  • Equations become MathML, which a screen reader can speak. A model reads each equation and proposes LaTeX. Checks then compare it with the PDF's own text.
  • Headings become real headings, with levels that make sense as an outline.
  • Lists become real lists.
  • Figures get short alt text, or a plain flag that they need a description.
  • Pages it cannot read, such as scans and handwriting, are kept as images and marked.

Anything it is unsure about goes to a review screen. The instructor accepts it, edits it or keeps the original. The examples on this site show that screen in a static form.

Who it is for

  • Instructors with a course PDF that has equations, who need a version a screen reader can use.
  • Accessibility staff who review such conversions and want to see what was changed and why.
  • Technical reviewers who want to see the output and the evidence behind it.

What it does not do

  • It does not check or certify conformance with WCAG, the ADA or any other standard. The output still needs a person to look at it.
  • It does not read scans or handwriting. Those pages are kept as images and flagged.
  • It is not a general PDF-to-Markdown converter. It starts from a PDF, not from Word, LaTeX or PowerPoint.
  • It does not remove the need for review. A model can be wrong, and some wrong answers agree with the PDF's text and are not flagged.

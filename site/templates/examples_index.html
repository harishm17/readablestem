How to read an example

  • Decisions asked counts the things the tool would ask a person to decide. A group of repeated headings counts once.
  • An item the tool accepts without asking can still be wrong. The decisions shown are the tool's choice of where a person should look first.
  • The PDFs of the OpenStax pages were printed from OpenStax's web pages by this project, not taken from the publisher's own PDFs.

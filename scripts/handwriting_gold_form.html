How to fill this in
  1. Drag a rectangle around one handwritten expression on the page. A box appears on the right.
  2. Type the LaTeX for it yourself, from what you see. There is no draft. The preview is only a check that it parses.
  3. Pick "display" for an equation on its own line, "inline" for one inside a sentence.
  4. If you cannot tell what the ink says, tick "can't tell" and type your best guess. These are left out of the main score.
  5. Conventions: \bar{x} for a bar over one letter; \text{...} for words inside maths; \left( \right) for brackets that grow; \begin{pmatrix} for matrices.
  6. Everything is saved on this computer as you type. When every expression on every page of a PDF is boxed and typed, tick "This PDF is finished".

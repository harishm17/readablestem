Privacy

Draft, to be reviewed by Harish. This page describes how the beta actually behaves today; the wording is not final.

Who runs this

a11y-stem is a free, open-source beta run by Harish, an individual developer, not a company or a university.{% if contact_email %} Contact: {{ contact_email }}.{% endif %}

What you may upload

Course materials you wrote yourself, or have the right to adapt, such as lecture notes, slides and problem sets. Do not upload student records, graded work, or anything else with students' personal information in it.

What is stored, and for how long

For each upload we store: the PDF and its file name, images cut from it (equations and figures), the conversion result (proposed LaTeX, MathML, alt text and headings), the converted HTML, and your review decisions.

  • All of it is deleted automatically {{ retention_hours }} hours after upload. From that moment it can no longer be opened or downloaded; an hourly clean-up then removes it from the database, normally within the hour.
  • If you tick "Keep my document and results for {{ opt_in_days }} days to help improve the tool", it is deleted after {{ opt_in_days }} days instead. During that time Harish may look at the conversion results to find and fix mistakes in the tool. The box is unticked unless you tick it.
  • You can delete a document and everything made from it at any time with "Delete now". It is removed from the database straight away.

Your account

You sign in with Google{% if microsoft_on %} or with Microsoft (a school or work account){% endif %}. We receive your email address, your name, and whether the address is verified. For people on the beta list we store the email address, name, the time of the last sign-in, and the id your sign-in provider gives your account{% if microsoft_on %} (for Microsoft, also your organisation's directory id){% endif %}, so we know it is you next time. If you sign in and are not on the list, nothing is stored about you.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in (for up to 7 days) and protects forms against forgery. There are no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

What is logged

Logs record what happened, not what was in your document: event names, random ids, page and fix counts, timings, model costs and error categories (for example unknown_command). They never contain document text, images, LaTeX, alt text, file names or your email address. Before documents are deleted, daily counts (for example, how many equations needed review) are added to totals that contain no content.

How it is protected

{# Every line below is a behaviour the tests exercise (docs/security.md, tests/test_audit_*.py); keep it that way when editing. #}
  • The site is served over HTTPS only.
  • Only you can open your documents. Other people on the beta, and the people who run it, are told "not found" for an address that is yours.
  • The converted document is shown in a sandbox that stops it running scripts.
  • An uploaded PDF is first opened in a separate short-lived process with its own time and memory limits, and is kept only in the database, never as a file on the server's disk.
  • Sign-in, uploads and daily page counts have limits that stop automated misuse.

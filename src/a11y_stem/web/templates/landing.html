Turn STEM course PDFs into accessible HTML

a11y-stem assists instructors with making their course materials accessible. Upload a PDF of your own notes, slides or problem sets, and get back one HTML file where:

  • equations are MathML, which screen readers can read aloud and let students explore,
  • headings are real headings, in a repaired outline,
  • figures have short alt text, or a clear flag where a description is still needed.

Every change comes with a confidence score and the automatic checks it passed. Anything uncertain goes to a review screen, where you accept, edit or reject it. The tool assists you; it does not replace your judgement or a full accessibility review of your course.

Who can use it

The beta is open to a small group of instructors, by invitation. Upload only materials you wrote or have the right to adapt, and never upload student records or anything with students' personal information in it.

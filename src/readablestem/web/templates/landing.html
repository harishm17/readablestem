What happens to your documents

Your file and everything made from it are deleted {{ retention_hours }} hours after you last work on it. You can keep a document for 7 days, or let us look at the results for {{ opt_in_days }} days to find mistakes in the tool. You can delete it at any time. We do not use your uploads to train models. Read the privacy notice.

Why STEM courses need this now

The Department of Justice's ADA Title II web rule asks public universities to make online course materials accessible. In STEM the hard part is the maths: a PDF equation is usually a picture to a screen reader. What the rule asks.

For accessibility and disability services offices

ReadableSTEM assists the instructor. It does not replace your office's review, and it does not say that a document meets WCAG, the ADA or Section 508. Where a document goes, who receives it and a pilot checklist.

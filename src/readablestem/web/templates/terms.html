What it does, and what it does not

The tool assists you in making course materials more accessible: it proposes MathML for equations, a repaired heading outline and short alt text for figures, each with a confidence score. Proposals can be wrong. Check the output, using the review screen, before you share it with students. The tool does not certify that a document meets any accessibility standard or law, and it does not replace an accessibility review.

The service

ReadableSTEM is a free beta, open by invitation to a small group of instructors. It may change, pause or stop at any time. Daily limits apply to pages and uploads, and the service pauses for the day when its budget for AI model calls is used up.

What it does, and what it does not

The tool assists you in making course materials more accessible: it proposes MathML for equations, a repaired heading outline and short alt text for figures, each with a confidence score. Proposals can be wrong. Check the output, using the review screen, before you share it with students. The tool does not certify that a document meets any accessibility standard or law, and it does not replace an accessibility review.

Your materials

  • Upload only materials you wrote or have the right to adapt.
  • Never upload student records, graded work or anything with students' personal information in it.
  • You keep all rights to your materials and to the converted output. By uploading, you let the service process them to produce the conversion, and keep them for the time described in the privacy notice.

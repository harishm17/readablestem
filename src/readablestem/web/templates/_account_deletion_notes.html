What deleting does not reach.

  • The database is backed up once a day, and each backup is kept for about 6 days, so your account and documents can remain in a backup for up to about a week after you delete them. Backups are only used to recover the service after a failure.
  • The images and nearby text of equations and figures that were already sent to {% if provider == "anthropic" %}Anthropic stay under its own retention: its published policy is to delete API inputs and outputs within 30 days, longer for material its safety systems flag{% else %}OpenAI stay under its own retention: see OpenAI's API data policies{% endif %}. Nothing in them names you.

  • For the rest of today we keep a scrambled fingerprint of your address, with today's page and read counts, so that deleting an account and signing up again does not reset today's limits. It is deleted tomorrow.

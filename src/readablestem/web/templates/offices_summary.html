ReadableSTEM: summary for accessibility offices

Facts as they were on {{ as_of }}. This describes the beta; it is not a contract or an audit. Open the live page, which may be newer.

ReadableSTEM assists instructors who turn a STEM course PDF into an accessible web page: equations as MathML, real headings{% if tables_on %}, tables{% endif %} and alt text for figures. The result is a web page, with {% if word_export %}Word, {% endif %}EPUB and Canvas versions if wanted. The instructor reviews what the tool is unsure about. Built and maintained by Harish Manoharan; the code is open source (source code).

What is sent where

The instructor uploads a PDF over HTTPS to the app, hosted by Railway in the United States. Students do not sign in. The PDF file, its file name and the instructor's email address are never sent to {{ model_names }}.

Deleting by hand is immediate.

Student records (FERPA)

  • Only course material the instructor wrote or may adapt is to be uploaded. The terms forbid student records, graded work and students' personal information.
  • Processed: the course material, and the instructor's email, name and sign-in id. Not processed: student names, grades, submissions or accounts.
  • No school-official or data processing agreement is offered.

What it does not certify

  • It does not say that a document meets WCAG, the ADA, Section 508 or any other standard or law. Proposals can be wrong: in a pre-registered test on 26 documents, 0.79 of the equations were exactly right end to end, against 0.77 for a general chatbot, and the tool asked about 46% of its wrong equations.
  • Its checks look at structure, not meaning. It has not been tested by listening with NVDA, JAWS or VoiceOver. No accessibility conformance report (VPAT) is published yet (one is in preparation) and there has been no independent security test.
  • Scanned and handwritten pages convert only on request, and a person checks them. Chemistry is the weakest area.

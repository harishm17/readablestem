ReadableSTEM: summary for accessibility offices

Facts as they were on {{ as_of }}. The live version is at {{ site_url }}{{ page_path }}. This describes the beta; it is not a contract or an audit.

ReadableSTEM assists instructors who turn a STEM course PDF into an accessible web page: equations as MathML, real headings, alt text for figures, one HTML file. The instructor reviews what the tool is unsure about. Built and maintained by Harish Manoharan; the code is open source ({{ source_url }}).

Where a document goes

The instructor uploads a PDF over HTTPS to the app, hosted by Railway in the United States. Images of equations and figures{% if tables_on %} and tables{% endif %}, with a little nearby text, are sent to {{ model_names }}. The PDF file, its file name and the instructor's email address are not sent. Students do not sign in.

Retention and training

  • Deleted {{ retention_hours }} hours after the instructor last works on it; 7 days if the instructor keeps it; {{ opt_in_days }} days if the instructor also lets the developer look. Deleting by hand is immediate. Database backups keep a deleted document up to about a week.
  • Uploads are not used to train any model. {{ model_names }} states that it does not train on API data by default and keeps API data for a limited time; see its own policy. {% if model_company == "OpenAI" %}Requests are sent with store off.{% else %}The store setting is for the OpenAI path only.{% endif %}

Student records (FERPA)

Only course material the instructor wrote or may adapt is to be uploaded. Student records, graded work and students' personal information are forbidden by the terms. Processed: the course material and the instructor's email, name and sign-in id. Not processed: student names, grades, submissions or accounts. No school-official or data processing agreement is offered.

What it does not certify

  • It does not say that a document meets WCAG, the ADA, Section 508 or any other standard or law. Proposals can be wrong: in a pre-registered test on 15 textbook sections, about half of the equations (0.50) were exactly right end to end.
  • Its checks look at structure, not meaning. Not tested by listening with NVDA, JAWS or VoiceOver. No accessibility conformance report (VPAT) and no independent security test yet.
  • Scanned and handwritten pages convert only on request and a person checks them. Chemistry is the weakest area.

Pilot in eight steps

1 One course, one document, no student records. 2 Read the privacy notice with your privacy contact. 3 The instructor uploads and reviews every decision. 4 Check the file with your screen readers. 5 Check it with your usual checker and review; no result is claimed. 6 Post it in the course and open it as a student. 7 Download the file and the review record before deletion. 8 Report what was wrong.

{# How a document is deleted, and what deleting does not reach: the security page and the downloadable retention summary (retention_summary.html) both include this, so the two cannot differ. `backup_days` is in the context of both. #}
  • A document past its time answers “not found” at once, and an hourly job deletes it and what was made from it. “Delete now” removes the same rows at once, after a confirm page.
  • Deleting an account removes the person's documents, courses, events and sign-in. A scrambled fingerprint of the address stays until the next day so the day's limits do not reset, and one line of a one-way hash of the account id and the time stays so a restored backup can delete the account again.
  • Not reached by deleting: database backups keep a copy for about {{ backup_days }} days, and the model provider's own retention is its own.

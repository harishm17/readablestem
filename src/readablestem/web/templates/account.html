How long your documents are kept

A document is deleted {{ retention_hours }} hours after you last work on it: the clock starts again each time you make a decision or download it. You can choose “Keep for 7 days” instead, when you upload a document or later from its page.

The “Keep for 7 days” box on the upload form then starts ticked, and you can untick it for any upload. Documents you already uploaded keep the deletion time they have.

Delete my account

This deletes your account and everything in it: your email address and name, every document you uploaded and everything made from it, your review decisions, courses and notation, and the notes about what you did in the app. It cannot be undone.
